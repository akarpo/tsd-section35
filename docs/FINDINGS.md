Compiled 2026-11-18. Labels: **[DOC]** the document says it; **[CALC]** our arithmetic, shown;
**[NOT FOUND]** searched, nothing found. State FY runs Oct 1 – Sep 30 ("FY2024" = 2023-24); Troy's

## 1. Bottom line on 35j

**Confirmed: Troy received no Section 35j money.**
- 35j was a one-time $140 million grant in the FY2024 budget (2023 PA 103, Sec. 35j). Only
  districts that **applied** in MDE's single NexSys window (Jan 26 – Feb 23, 2024) got it, at an
  equal $311.08 per pre-K–5 pupil, half in FY2024 and half in FY2025. [DOC: PA103; MDE memo; MDE report]
- Troy's status-report pages show **no 35j line** in FY2024 (p. 1124) or FY2025 (p. 1127). Oakland
  Schools, which could have applied on Troy's behalf, received none (FY2024 p. 1095). [DOC]
- 22 of Oakland County's 28 local districts received it. Non-recipients: Troy, Pontiac,
  Clarenceville, South Lyon, Oak Park, Clawson. [CALC from the status reports]
- **Estimated share:** 5,217 PK-5 pupils (NCES fall 2022) × $311.08 = $1,622,904; about $1,604,300
  after the dilution of a fixed pot. [CALC]

**Likely, not documented: Troy did not apply.** MDE told the Legislature that "434 LEAs applied for
and received funding" and mentions no denials. No Troy board document mentions 35j. [NOT FOUND]
A FOIA request to Troy or MDE for 35j application records would settle it.

**Unrecoverable.** The work project ended Sept. 30, 2025; Sec. 35j was repealed by 2025 PA 15
(immediate effect Oct. 7, 2025). [DOC]

## 2. The Section 35 subsections that matter for Troy

- **35a(5), additional instructional time:** $19.9M a year FY2019–FY2025, by application, equal per
  first-grade pupil; eliminated in FY2026 (HFA: "Eliminates $19.9 million SAF"). Troy's only
  recurring Section 35 grant.
- **35a(4), ISD early-literacy coaches:** to ISDs only. Oakland Schools received $3,000,000 a year
  FY2024–FY2026. Not Troy revenue.
- **35a(10), LETRS professional learning:** paid to the training provider through an ISD; districts
  get free training. Not Troy revenue.
- **35j:** see section 1.
- **35m, literacy materials (Committee for Literacy Achievement):** $87.0M in FY2025, $70.0M in
  FY2026, $50.0M in FY2027 (formula grant; applications Dec. 2026). Troy: $1,120,928 (2024-25 award,
  UFLI Foundations, paid as a prior-year adjustment in FY2026); $1,173,876 recommended for 2025-26,
  **not paid as of the Nov. 20, 2026 status report**, when other Oakland districts' 2026 awards were
  paid (e.g., Birmingham $720,411). MDE pays 35m after the district's NexSys budget is approved.
- **35d, dyslexia / Orton-Gillingham training:** $1.0M in FY2022–FY2025; in FY2024–FY2025 MDE paid
  the providers directly ($400,000 MDI, $600,000 IMSE) to train district staff free; dropped in
  FY2026 (HFA one-time items not continued); **$1,000,000 for 2026-27** (2026 PA 25 Sec. 35d) as
  first-come, first-served grants to districts and ISDs.

Full appropriations history by subsection (35a, 35b, 35c, 35d, 35g, 35i, 35j, 35m, 35n, 35p, 35q)
is in the HFA line-item summaries listed in section 7.

From the parsed status reports (re-checked against the PDF pages by `tools/build_data.py`); see
`public/assets/oakland-section35j.csv`. Each recipient's two-year total ÷ $311.08 equals its NCES

## 8. Every Section 35 grant, FY2016–FY2027 (added 2026-11-19)

The `/history` page and `sources/history/programs.json`. Research notes:

- **No Section 35 existed in the FY2012–FY2015 acts** (2011 PA 62, 2012 PA 201, 2013 PA 60,
  2014 PA 196 searched for "Sec. 35"). Sec. 35 and 35a were added by 2015 PA 85 (FY2016); MCL
  388.1635a's history line confirms "Add. 2015, Act 85". Sec. 35 (implementation money and the
  NAEP goal) was repealed by 2018 PA 265, which moved the goal into 35a(1).
- **Letters are reused.** 35d: FY2020 social-emotional learning pilot (vetoed), FY2021–FY2025
  Orton-Gillingham/dyslexia training (repealed by 2025 PA 15), FY2027 dyslexia teacher training
  (new). 35e: FY2021–FY2022 Boys and Girls Clubs; FY2026 Wayne State school library study
  (repealed by 2026 PA 25, imd. eff. July 21, 2026). 35a's subsections are renumbered often (the
  coaches were 35a(5), then (4), now (2)).
- **Supplementals and vetoes that changed amounts:** 2016 PA 249 (June 2016) cut FY2016 35a and
  dropped the parent pilots; 2018 PA 586 (Dec. 2018) added $1.0M for the smallest ISD (Literacy
  Essentials), raised MEC to $3.0M and redirected 35b to the Children's Choice Initiative; the
  governor's Sept. 30, 2019 vetoes and 2019 PA 162 (Dec. 2019) set FY2020 (HFA FY20 summary).
  Searched the Sec. 35 text of every later act for amendments to a prior year's amount: none.
- **HFA discrepancy:** HFA's FY2015-16 line-item summary says "$3.5 million for teacher coaches";
  2015 PA 85 says $3,000,000.00 and the SAF total ($23.9M) only adds up with $3.0M. The page uses
  the act.
- **Paid outside state aid** (so not in the payment reports): Michigan Education Corps, 35b,
  35d, 35e, 35f, 35h, the summer-slide grant, the certification test, LETRS before FY2026, and
  Literacy Essentials in FY2019–FY2020 (paid to no one in the reports; how it was paid is not
  documented here).
- **35m payments vs. MDE's count:** MDE says 561 districts were funded for 2024-25; the Aug. 20,
  2026 final report shows 468 districts/ISDs paid on 2024-25 awards ($79,879,422.12). The rest may
  be paid later; not checked.
- **35j:** the payment reports show 416 entities in FY2024 and 401 in FY2025 (MDE: 434 and 420), net
  FY2024 $67,780,747 after $1,995,428 taken back in later reports.
- **Retrieval:** legislature.mi.gov started refusing connections after ~40 requests in a few
  minutes (11/19). 2015 PA 85, 2016 PA 249, 2017 PA 108, 2019 PA 58 and 2026 PA 25 came from the
  Wayback Machine (`web.archive.org/web/2026id_/<url>`); 2019 PA 162 and 2025 PA 15 returned 403
  there. HFA documents (house.mi.gov) were always reachable. Fetch one file at a time.
