Literacy Grants Fiscal Year 2027

Please find below updates for the fiscal year 2027 literacy grants. Fiscal year 2027 runs October 1, 2026 - September 30, 2027. Please note all Section 35 grants allow for one year of carryover

Section 35a(2) ISD Early Literacy Coach Grant

The 2026-27 Section 35a(2) ISD Early Literacy Coach Grant application will open in NexSys by the end of November 2026.

Each ISD is eligible to receive $125,000 per coach and must have a minimum of 4 early literacy coaches. Each ISD that already meets the 4-coach minimum is eligible to maintain their FY 26 allocation and automatically receive 1 additional coaching allocation. All additional coaching allocations will be distributed to ISDs based on the percentage of total statewide number of pupils in grades K to 3 who meet the income eligibility standards for the federal free and reduced-price lunch programs who are enrolled in districts within the ISD. ISDs eligible for additional funding will be contacted by MDE after the application closes.

Section 35a(4) LETRS Training Grant

Section 35a(4) of the FY 2027 State School Aid Act provides $75,000,000 to an intermediate district identified by the department for the provision of professional learning by the approved provider described in subsection (5)(Lexia Learning) to provide professional learning described in subsection (5)(LETRS) to Pre-K to grade 5 educators that provide, support, or oversee pre-K to grade 5 reading instruction; special education educators; administrators overseeing Pre-K to grade 5 educators that provide, support, or oversee pre-K to grade 5 reading instruction and special education educators; literacy coaches; faculty and staff at educator preparation programs that teach reading fundamental course and partners that lead regional and statewide literacy professional development, as determined by the department.

To learn more about LETRS training, please visit the Michigan LETRS website.

Section 35m Committee for Literacy Achievement/HQ Literacy Materials Grant

Section 35m of the FY 2027 State School Aid Act provides $50,000,000 to the Michigan Department of Education (MDE) to continue the Committee for Literacy Achievement and provide funding to districts and intermediate districts based on a rankings list of materials. The Committee for Literacy Achievement’s primary focus is to recommend tools and strategies with the goal of increasing student academic outcomes in the area of literacy.

The 2026-27 Section 35m Committee for Literacy Achievement / HQ Literacy Materials Grant Application will open in NexSys in December 2026. Districts will choose one category: Tier 1 (Pre-K to 5), Assessments (K-3), Interventions (Pre-K to 5) or Professional Development (Pre-K to 12).

Please Note: This is a formula Grant that includes both a base allocation and a Pre-K to 12 per-pupil amount (based on fall 2026 certified count).

After the MDE has determined the per-pupil formula based on the number of applicants, districts and Intermediate School Districts (ISDs) will be notified through NexSys that modifications are required. Modifications will be required for all Applications. This step will be the entry point to allow districts and ISDs to create their budget in NexSys for approval.

For more information and updates pertaining to this Grant, please visit our Committee for Literacy Achievement website.

Section 35d Dyslexia Orton Gillingham Training Grant

The 26-27 Section 35d Dyslexia Orton Gillingham Program Grant application will open for districts in NexSys in spring 2027.

Districts will apply on a first-come, first-served basis until all funds are allocated. In the application, districts will choose their preferred provider that meets the criteria outlined in legislation, upload a quote or purchase order from the provider, identify the number of staff to be trained, and funds will be awarded accordingly for the training.

Professional Learning | Michigan Virtual 

In collaboration with Michigan Virtual, the Office of Curriculum and Instruction, Literacy Unit, is pleased to announce a new self-paced virtual course for educators, Introduction to Michigan's K–12 Literacy & Dyslexia Law | Michigan Virtual. This course introduces the characteristics of dyslexia, highlights potential reading difficulties that may signal a student needs additional literacy support, and explores practical instructional adjustments grounded in the science of reading. Participants are able to earn 2-general SCECH for completion of the course at no cost.  

Celebrating American Education Week: Inspiring Literacy Across Michigan

November 16–20, 2026

Strong readers build strong futures! During American Education Week, Michigan celebrates the educators, families, and school support professionals who help students learn, grow, and succeed.

First observed in 1921 to address national literacy concerns, this annual celebration reminds us that literacy is a shared responsibility. Schools can encourage reading activities, recognize student achievements, and celebrate education professionals. Families and guardians can support literacy at home by reading together, exploring books, and making reading part of everyday life.

Together, we can inspire a lifelong love of reading and strengthen literacy for every Michigan student.

Learn more: National Education Association – American Education Week
