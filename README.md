# tsd-section35

**Live:** <https://tsd-section35.karpowitsch.org>

An independent, source-linked look at Troy School District and Michigan's Section 35 literacy
grants (State School Aid Act). It confirms that Troy received none of Section 35j, the one-time
$140 million literacy grant of 2023–24 (22 of Oakland County's 28 local districts did), lists every
Section 35 dollar Troy has received since 2015–16, explains the new $1 million Section 35d
Orton-Gillingham grant, and asks the district questions. A second page, **`/history`**
(`public/history.html`), describes every grant Michigan has written into Section 35 since it began
in 2015–16 (27 grants in 12 budgets): what each paid for, who could get it, the amount each year,
who was paid, and Troy's share.

**Picking this up in a new session? Start with [`HANDOFF.md`](HANDOFF.md)**: current state, open
items, and the mistakes not to repeat.

## Rules for changes

- Every number on the main page appears in `docs/FIGURES.md` with a source. Add the row first.
  On `/history`, add a grant-year to `programs.json` with its quote; add any payment figure you
  put in prose as a `facts` entry and any document fact as a `checks` entry, then run
  `python -I tools/build_history.py`.
- Chart and CSV data come from `tools/build_data.py`; never type them by hand.
- The status-report PDFs scramble their amount columns when converted to text. Render the page
  (PyMuPDF `get_pixmap`) and read it before quoting an amount; the same goes for MDE's 35m lists.
- Estimates are ours and stay labeled ("our estimate", "about").
- "Troy didn't apply" is an inference from MDE's wording, not a documented fact. Keep it labeled
  that way unless the district or a FOIA response says otherwise.
- After any edit, run `python -I tools/qa.py` (1280 px and 390 px, light and dark) and look at the
  screenshots; the script catches overflow and heading order.

## Hosting

Cloudflare Pages project `tsd-section35`, Git-connected to `akarpo/tsd-section35` (`main`), custom
domain `tsd-section35.karpowitsch.org` (proxied CNAME to the project's `pages.dev` host). A push to
`main` deploys. Never run `wrangler pages deploy`. After a push,
`python -I tools/cloudflare.py status` shows whether the build succeeded and
`python -I tools/qa.py --live` checks the result.
